# Jaberwocky

Track a cave monster, fire crossbow stingers, or seal caverns with dynamite.

The website plays v01, tape20a save 3, through the real TRS-80 ROM. Instructions are in the player Help and below its field. Original (1×) is the default; Fast (20×) is optional.

One complete captured save. Its room dispatcher and descriptions borrow from Roger Chaffee’s Quest. No direct source date or relative order with Alien is established.
