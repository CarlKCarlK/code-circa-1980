# German tutor

Practice German verbs and vocabulary with a configurable original quiz.

Inferred grouping by source features and lessons, not tape numbering: early vocabulary forms, timed vocabulary/template forms, then vocabulary and verb forms with configurable grading. Different lessons are not necessarily successive revisions. The two tape19b verb saves are the selected developed form; exact cross-tape chronology remains uncertain.
