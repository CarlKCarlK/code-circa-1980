# Speech

Coach a speech team: recruit students, assign events, and plan a tournament season.

Shown only in the unlinked second gallery (`#/more`). The player runs v10 with its printed spelling corrected (`corrected/`) through the TRS-80 emulator; the recovered files are unchanged.

Inferred: eight complete tape20a drafts in save order, then tape11a save 12 (expanded introduction and team assignment), then tape11b saves 7 and 8 (new title animation, event uniqueness check and corrected award lookup). The last two are repeated saves. Tape numbers alone do not establish chronology. Damaged draft 02 and draft 10 remain separate; later malformed material is not treated as a complete version.

All recovered snapshots are retained in `versions/`. `original/` advances through
individual commits and program tags; its latest historical snapshot may differ
from the playable default. Hashes, archive links, and inventory evidence are in
`provenance.json`. Recovery validation did not include execution testing. See
`VERIFICATION.md` for new website checks.
