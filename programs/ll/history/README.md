# Source history and dating

Comparison history starts with Jim Storer’s 1969 FOCAL listing, then an electronic transcription of the published BASIC Lunar. The recovered BASIC adaptation adds plotting and adjustable duration; its exact intermediate source edition and save date are unknown.

The full original FOCAL listing is downloaded unchanged from Jim Storer’s own history archive, which credits Matthew House’s transcription. This is the first canonical source commit, ll-storer1969. A complete electronic transcription of published BASIC Lunar follows as ll-basic1978, before untouched tape9a save 9 at ll-v01. The original FOCAL, published BASIC, and recovered adaptation share state variables and polynomial physics. The exact intermediate BASIC edition used by Carl is unknown. The recovered record 1710 omits the published branch’s GOSUB physics call; the position polynomial also lacks the final Q^5 term. Do not claim numerical identity or repair those differences.

The other graphics-extension Lunar Landing programs and the separate Lunar Landing Simulator remain separate programs, not silently treated as revisions of this Lunar Lander.
